What is Evaluating?

  • Evaluating is not quite the same as regulating. Regulating is what you do "live" in the moment (while studying). Evaluating is what you do after you have evidence about whether or not you were successful. Obviously, this includes reflecting on your learning17!
  • Tracking your performance, and understanding why you have made mistakes allows you to quickly recognize when study strategies aren't working and initiate change when necessary.
