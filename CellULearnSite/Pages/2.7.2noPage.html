It's ok to experience failure!

Processing Failure: You are not your grade:

Failure to achieve your goal does not mean that YOU are a failure! Failure is a normal part of learning, and can provide an opportunity to figure out what you will do differently next time.

How to move forward:

  • Now that you have a clearer understanding of how assessments in this class are structured, it's time to reassess your plan of action and strategies.
  • Remind yourself that you are capable and can learn from and fix your mistakes.
  • Work with a learning partner to diagnose where you are getting off track.
  • Reach out to your instructor to discuss any misunderstandings and new strategies.
  • Empower yourself: now that you know what was challenging and why, you can make a plan to be more successful next time.

Establish a growth mindset. You are capable of doing better! Don't listen to internal voices that undermine your confidence. Instead, you can say to yourself some of the following19:

  • When I learn how to do a new kind of problem, it's growing my brain!
  • If I feel like I am not good at something, add a "yet" to the end. I can get better!
  • The point isn't to get it all right away - the point is to grow my understanding. What can I try next? What kind of effort will help me get better at what I want to learn?

Remember your aspirations and let your goals inspire you to keep going, even when things don't go as planned!
